About Zong Quiz Today

Zong Quiz Today is an independent guide website for Zong mobile users in Pakistan. We publish simple, practical information about the Zong daily quiz, USSD codes, data bundles, call packages and the My Zong app.

Our goal is to help users understand how these services work, practise for the daily quiz, and choose bundles that fit their needs — without confusing jargon. Every article on this site is written in plain language and is based on widely published information about Zong services.

This website is unofficial and independent. We are not affiliated with Zong, Zong CMPak Ltd, or any other telecom company, and we do not represent Zong in any way. The official source for codes, prices, packages and rewards is the My Zong app and Zong's own official channels.

Quiz questions in the My Zong app change every day, so we do not publish daily answers. Instead, we explain how the quiz works and how to prepare for it. Prices and offers change often, so always confirm the current details in the official My Zong app before buying anything.

If you have a question or a suggestion, you can reach us at contact@zongquiztoday.github.io.
