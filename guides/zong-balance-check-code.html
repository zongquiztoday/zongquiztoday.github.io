Zong Balance Check Code

Checking your balance is one of the most common things Zong users do. Whether you have just recharged, bought a bundle or played the daily quiz, you want to know how much balance is left. The quickest way is the USSD code *222#, but the My Zong app gives you even more detail. This guide covers both methods and what to do when they do not work.

Check balance with *222#

Open your phone's dialler, type *222# and press the call button. Within a few seconds, a message appears on your screen showing your remaining balance. The reply usually also shows the expiry date of your balance, which is useful to know before it runs out.

This method works on any phone, including basic feature phones, and it does not need the internet. A small service fee may be charged for the balance check — this is shown by Zong and can change, so confirm the current fee in the My Zong app if it matters to you.

Check balance in the My Zong app

Open the My Zong app and log in with your Zong number. Your current balance is shown on the home screen, along with your remaining data, minutes and SMS. Tap on the balance or usage section to see more detail, such as how much data you have used and when your bundles expire.

The app is the better option when you want the full picture. Unlike the *222# message, it shows everything in one place: balance, data, minutes, active bundles and their expiry dates. It also shows offers and rewards you may have missed.

Other useful check codes

Zong provides several short codes for checking different things. Dial *8# to see your own Zong number on screen — handy if you have forgotten it. Dial *102# to check your remaining internet data. Dial *6464# to see details of the package currently active on your number. These codes are widely published by Zong, but if any of them does not work on your number, check the My Zong app instead, because codes can change or may not apply to every account type.

If the balance check does not work

If *222# gives no reply, first check that you have a Zong signal — look for the network bars at the top of your screen. Try again after a minute, and restart your phone if the problem continues. Make sure you dialled the code exactly: star, 222, hash. If you are roaming or using a different SIM slot, switch to the Zong SIM and try again.

If the app does not show your balance, check your internet connection, update the app, and log out and back in. If both methods fail, call Zong support on 310 from your Zong number and ask them to check your balance for you.

Keeping track of your balance

Check your balance regularly, especially before buying a bundle or making a long call. Set a habit of checking once a day in the app, and note the expiry dates of your balance and bundles so nothing runs out unexpectedly. If your balance drops faster than expected, look at your usage in the app — background app updates and video streaming are the most common causes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Zong balance check code?

*222#. Dial it from your Zong number and your remaining balance appears on screen.

Can I check my balance without the code?

Yes — the My Zong app shows your balance, data, minutes and bundle expiry on the home screen.

Why is *222# not working?

Check your signal, dial the code exactly, and restart your phone. If it still fails, call Zong support on 310.
