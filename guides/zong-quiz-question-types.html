Zong Quiz Question Types and Preparation

The Zong daily quiz does not ask the same kind of question every time. Some questions test facts about Pakistan, others test science, sports or everyday knowledge. Knowing the types of questions in advance helps you prepare in the right way, instead of revising blindly. This guide breaks down the main question types and how to get ready for each.

Pakistan general knowledge

This is the most common category. Questions cover the capital, provinces, major cities, rivers, mountains, national symbols and famous places. Examples: the capital of Pakistan, the largest province by area, the longest river, or the national animal. These are school-level facts, and they are worth revising first because they appear so often.

Make a short list of key facts — capitals, rivers, mountains, national symbols, important dates — and revise it once a week. Our practice quiz on the homepage covers many of these facts.

History and famous personalities

History questions often ask about the Pakistan Movement, Independence Day, the founder of Pakistan, and important historical events and dates. Questions about famous personalities may cover poets, scientists, sports stars and leaders. Knowing the main names and dates — 14 August 1947, Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, Allama Iqbal — covers a large share of these questions.

When revising history, focus on the most famous events and people first. Quiz writers usually choose well-known facts rather than obscure details, so a solid grasp of the basics goes a long way.

Science and everyday knowledge

Science questions are usually simple and practical: the boiling point of water, the planet closest to the sun, the chemical symbol for water, or how many legs an insect has. Everyday knowledge questions cover topics like the number of days in a leap year, the colours of the rainbow, or basic health and food facts.

These questions reward general awareness rather than deep study. Reading science facts for children or general-knowledge books is a good way to prepare, and many of the answers are things you already know but have not thought about recently.

Sports and current affairs

Sports questions often focus on cricket, Pakistan's most popular sport: famous players, World Cup history, and basic rules. Football and hockey appear too. Current affairs questions cover recent news — new government announcements, major sports results, and international events. Reading the news for a few minutes a day is the best preparation for this category, because these questions change with the headlines.

Islamic studies and culture

Questions about Islamic studies may ask about the number of Surahs in the Quran, the months of the Islamic calendar, or basic facts about Islamic history. Culture questions may cover Pakistani food, languages, festivals and traditions. These are usually straightforward for players who know the basics.

How to prepare for all of them

Do not try to memorise everything at once. Instead, revise one category per day: Pakistan facts on Monday, history on Tuesday, science on Wednesday, and so on. Use our practice quiz regularly, read the news, and keep a small notebook of facts you get wrong. If you miss a question in the practice quiz, read the correct answer and think about why it is right, because the same fact often appears again in a different form. After a few weeks, you will notice the same topics coming up again and again — and your scores will climb.

Frequently asked questions

What topics appear in the Zong quiz?

Mostly Pakistan general knowledge, history, science, sports, current affairs, Islamic studies and culture.

How should I prepare for the quiz?

Revise one topic per day, read the news, and practise with multiple-choice quizzes like the one on our homepage.

Are the questions very difficult?

No — most are school-level general knowledge. The challenge is answering quickly and accurately.
