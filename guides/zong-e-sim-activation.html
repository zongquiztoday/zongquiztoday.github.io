Zong eSIM: How to Activate and Use It

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into your phone. Instead of inserting a small plastic card, you activate your mobile plan by scanning a QR code or entering details in your phone's settings. Zong supports eSIM for compatible phones, and it is a convenient option if your phone has no free SIM slot or you want to use two numbers at once. This guide explains how to get and activate a Zong eSIM.

What is an eSIM?

A traditional SIM is a physical chip you put into your phone. An eSIM does the same job, but it is a small chip already built into the phone, and your mobile plan is loaded onto it digitally. Your number, calls, SMS and data work exactly the same as with a physical SIM — the only difference is how the plan is installed.

Many modern phones support eSIM, including recent iPhones and Samsung, Google Pixel and other Android phones. Older phones and budget models often do not. Check your phone's settings or manual to see whether it supports eSIM before you apply.

How to get a Zong eSIM

To get a Zong eSIM, you normally need to visit a Zong service centre or authorised franchise with your original CNIC. The staff verify your identity, as required by law, and issue the eSIM — usually as a QR code printed on a card or shown on a screen. Some new connections and SIM replacements can also be arranged through official Zong channels, but identity verification in person is usually required.

Ask the staff whether your current number and bundles will carry over to the eSIM. In most cases your number stays the same and your existing balance and bundles continue as normal, but confirm this before the switch.

Activating the eSIM on your phone

On an iPhone, go to Settings, then Mobile Service or Cellular, then Add eSIM, and scan the QR code. On Android, the path is usually Settings, then Connections or Network, then SIM Manager, then Add eSIM, and scan the QR code. Your phone will download the plan and ask you to confirm. Make sure you have a Wi-Fi connection during activation, because the phone needs the internet to download the eSIM profile.

Once the eSIM is installed, set it as your line for calls, SMS and data. If you also have a physical SIM in the phone, you can choose which SIM to use for each purpose, or use one for calls and the other for data.

Using two numbers at once

One of the best things about eSIM is dual SIM use. Many phones can use a physical SIM and an eSIM at the same time, giving you two numbers in one phone. This is useful if you have a Zong number for personal use and another network for work, or if you travel and want to add a local eSIM while keeping your Zong number active for SMS.

Label each line clearly in your phone settings — for example "Zong Personal" and "Work" — so you always know which number you are calling or texting from.

If activation fails

If the QR code does not scan, check that the camera lens is clean and the code is well lit. Make sure your phone has an internet connection and that it supports eSIM. Do not delete the eSIM profile if activation fails — contact the service centre that issued it, because a QR code can usually only be used a limited number of times.

If the eSIM installs but you have no signal, restart your phone, check that the line is turned on in your settings, and confirm that the eSIM is selected for mobile data. If the problem continues, visit a Zong service centre or call 310 for help.
