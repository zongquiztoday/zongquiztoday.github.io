Zong Package Codes (Complete List)

Zong uses short USSD codes — numbers you dial like *222# — to check your balance, activate bundles and manage your account. This page lists the best-known codes in one place, with a short explanation of each. Use this list as a quick reference, and always confirm current codes and prices in the My Zong app, because codes and offers can change.

Balance and account codes

CodeWhat it does
*222#Check your remaining balance and its expiry.
*8#Show your own Zong mobile number on screen.
*102#Check your remaining internet / data balance.
*224#Check your remaining free minutes on eligible bundles.
*6464#View details of the package currently active on your number.
310Call Zong customer support (helpline, not a USSD code).

These are the most widely published Zong codes. If any of them does not work on your number, check the My Zong app instead — the app always shows your balance, data, minutes and active packages without needing a code.

How to use a USSD code

Open your phone's dialler app, type the code exactly as shown — including the star at the start and the hash at the end — and press the call button. A message will appear on your screen within a few seconds. Do not add spaces or extra digits, because the code will not work.

USSD codes work on any phone, including basic feature phones, and they do not need the internet. They are the most reliable way to check your account when you have no data connection. Some codes may carry a small service fee, which Zong shows in the reply or in the app.

Bundle subscription codes

Zong also provides USSD codes for subscribing to specific bundles. These codes are listed inside the My Zong app next to each bundle, and on Zong's official channels. Because bundle offers change regularly, we do not list subscription codes for individual bundles here — a code that worked last month may subscribe you to a different bundle today, or may no longer work at all.

The safest way to subscribe is through the My Zong app, where you can see the bundle name, data, minutes, validity and price before you confirm. If you prefer a code, copy it from the app itself, not from a random website.

Codes to be careful with

Never dial a code sent to you by a stranger in a message or on social media. Some codes can forward your calls, change your settings or subscribe you to a paid service. Only dial codes you got from the My Zong app, from Zong's official website, or from this kind of trusted guide.

Also be careful with codes that promise "free data" or "free balance" from unofficial sources. Genuine free data comes only through the official My Zong app — the daily quiz, check-ins and promotions. Any code that asks for money or personal details is a scam.

Keeping this list up to date

Zong changes its bundles and offers from time to time, and codes can change with them. Check the My Zong app whenever a code stops working, and treat this page as a starting point rather than a final authority. The app is always the most current source for your account.

Dial *102#, or check the balance section of the My Zong app.

Where can I find codes to subscribe to a bundle?

In the My Zong app, next to each bundle. Copy the code from the app, not from random websites.
