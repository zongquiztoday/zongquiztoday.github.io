My Zong App: Download and Use It for the Quiz

The My Zong app is the official self-service app for Zong users in Pakistan. It lets you check your balance, buy bundles, view your usage and play the daily quiz — all from your phone. If you want to play the quiz or claim daily rewards, this app is the place to do it. This guide walks you through downloading it, setting it up and finding your way around.

Downloading the app

On an Android phone, open the Google Play Store, search for "My Zong" and install the app published by Zong. On an iPhone, open the Apple App Store and do the same. Only install the app from these official stores. Apps with similar names from other websites may be fake and could harm your phone or steal your information.

The app is free to download. You will need a little mobile data or a Wi-Fi connection to install it, and enough free storage space on your phone. If the download is slow, switch to Wi-Fi and try again.

Logging in for the first time

Open the app and enter your Zong mobile number. The app will send a verification code to that number by SMS. Enter the code in the app to confirm that the number belongs to you. This step is important: never share the verification code with anyone, and never enter it on any other website or app.

Once you are logged in, the app will show your balance, your active bundles and your remaining data on the home screen. You may also be asked to allow notifications. Allowing them is useful, because the app uses notifications to tell you about rewards, bundle expiry and new offers.

Finding the daily quiz

After logging in, look for the quiz on the home screen. It usually appears as a banner, card or icon with a quiz or rewards theme. Its exact position can change when the app is updated, so if you do not see it immediately, scroll down the home screen or look in a rewards, offers or "play and win" section.

Tap the quiz card to open the game. Read the short instructions on the quiz screen — they tell you how many questions there are, whether there is a time limit, and what score you need for a reward. Then tap start and answer each question. Your result and any reward are normally shown right after you finish.

Other useful features

The app does much more than the quiz. You can check your remaining balance and data, see which bundles are active on your number, buy new bundles, and view your usage history. The app also shows special offers, daily check-in rewards and sometimes a spin wheel or bonus game. Browsing these sections once a day is a good habit — many users miss free data simply because they never open the offers page.

You can also manage more than one Zong number in the app if you have several SIMs, and you can recharge your balance through the app using a bank card or mobile wallet where supported.

If the app does not work

If the app crashes or will not open, first check that your phone has an internet connection and enough free storage. Then update the app to the latest version from the Play Store or App Store, close it completely and open it again. If the problem continues, clear the app's cache from your phone settings (Android) or delete and reinstall the app (iPhone and Android). As a last step, call Zong support on 310 and explain the problem.

Keep your app updated. Zong changes the quiz, rewards and offers regularly, and older versions of the app may not show the newest features at all.

Frequently asked questions

Is the My Zong app free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. You only use a small amount of data when it loads.

Where is the quiz inside the app?

Usually on the home screen as a banner or card, or in a rewards or offers section. Its position can change after app updates.

I did not receive the login code. What should I do?

Wait a minute, check your signal, and ask for the code again. If it still does not arrive, restart your phone or call 310.
