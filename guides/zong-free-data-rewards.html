Zong Free Data and Daily Rewards

Free data is one of the best things about the My Zong app. Zong regularly gives small amounts of free data and other bonuses to users who open the app, play the quiz or take part in promotions. None of these will replace a proper data bundle, but added together over a month they can give you a useful amount of extra data for free. This guide covers the official ways to earn them.

The daily quiz

The daily quiz is the best-known way to earn free data. Play the quiz in the My Zong app, answer the questions, and if you meet the score requirement you can earn a small data reward, often 50 MB or 100 MB. The exact reward changes from day to day and is shown in the app. Play every day, because a missed day is usually a missed reward.

Daily check-ins and app rewards

The My Zong app often has a daily check-in feature: open the app each day and tap the check-in button to collect a small bonus. Some days the bonus is data, other days it is minutes or a discount. Checking in every day in a row sometimes earns a bigger reward at the end of the streak, so try not to break the chain.

Look for other reward features too, such as spin wheels, scratch cards or special event games inside the app. These appear and disappear with promotions, so it is worth opening the app's rewards or offers section once a day to see what is new.

Promotions and special offers

Zong runs promotions around events such as Eid, Independence Day, cricket matches and new bundle launches. These can include bonus data when you recharge or subscribe to a bundle, double-data days, or extra rewards in the quiz. The offers are announced inside the app and on Zong's official social media pages. If an offer sounds too good to be true — such as "unlimited free data forever" — check it in the official app before believing it.

Referrals and new SIM offers

From time to time, Zong offers bonuses for inviting friends to the app or for activating a new SIM. The details and conditions change with each promotion, and they are always listed in the app. Only follow offers shown inside the official app or on Zong's verified pages, and never share your account details with someone who promises a referral bonus.

Using free data before it expires

Free data almost always comes with a short validity — often 24 hours. That means you should use it the same day you earn it, or it disappears. Check the validity shown in the reward message, and use the free data for browsing or messaging before it expires. To check your free data balance, dial *102# or look in the My Zong app.

Be careful with scams. No genuine Zong offer will ask you to send money, share a verification code, or install an app from outside the official app stores. If someone messages you promising free data in exchange for a code or payment, it is a scam — report it and ignore it.

Frequently asked questions

How can I get free Zong data every day?

Play the daily quiz and use the daily check-in in the My Zong app. Both give small free data rewards.

How long does free quiz data last?

Usually 24 hours. Check the validity in the reward message and use it the same day.

Are free data offers on WhatsApp real?

Most are scams. Only trust offers shown inside the official My Zong app or on Zong's verified pages.
