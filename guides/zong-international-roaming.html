Zong International Roaming Packages

Travelling abroad with your Zong SIM can be very useful — you keep your own number, receive important messages and stay reachable. But roaming charges can be high if you do not prepare. This guide explains how Zong international roaming works and how to use it without a surprise bill.

What is roaming?

Roaming means using your Zong SIM on another country's mobile network. When you land abroad, your phone connects to a local partner network, and you can make calls, send SMS and use data — but at roaming rates, which are different from your normal Pakistani rates. Roaming is usually much more expensive than a local SIM in the country you visit.

Before you travel, check whether roaming is active on your number. Some accounts need roaming activated in advance. You can check and activate it through the My Zong app or by calling the helpline 310 before you leave Pakistan.

Roaming bundles

Zong offers roaming bundles for popular destinations, usually covering a fixed amount of data, minutes and SMS for a fixed number of days. These bundles are almost always cheaper than using roaming at the standard pay-as-you-go rate. The available bundles depend on the country you are visiting, so check the My Zong app for the bundles for your destination before you travel.

Always confirm the current price in the My Zong app, because roaming bundles and their prices change, and the bundle for one country may not work in another. Buy the bundle before you leave Pakistan, so it is active the moment you land.

Tips to avoid a big bill

The most important tip is to turn off mobile data roaming until you need it. In your phone settings, there is a separate switch for data roaming — keep it off while you are travelling, and turn it on only when you want to use data. This stops apps from using data in the background and running up charges.

Use Wi-Fi whenever it is available — in hotels, airports and cafes. Download maps, tickets and important documents before you travel, so you do not need data to access them. Turn off automatic app updates and photo backup over mobile data while you are abroad.

Receiving calls and SMS abroad

Receiving SMS while roaming is often free or cheap, which is good news for bank verification codes and important alerts. Receiving calls can cost money, so keep incoming calls short and call back over Wi-Fi using an internet calling app when you can. Let family and friends know you are travelling, so they use WhatsApp or another internet app to reach you instead of calling your number directly.

Should you buy a local SIM instead?

For a long stay, a local SIM in the country you visit is usually much cheaper than roaming, especially for data. You can keep your Zong SIM in the phone for incoming SMS and calls, and use the local SIM for data. If you use a phone with two SIM slots, this is easy: put the local SIM in one slot and keep Zong in the other.

For a short trip of a few days, a Zong roaming bundle is more convenient, because you keep your own number for everything and do not need to buy and register a new SIM. Choose based on the length of your trip and how much data you need.

Frequently asked questions

How do I activate Zong roaming before travelling?

Check and activate it in the My Zong app or by calling 310 before you leave Pakistan.

Are Zong roaming bundles cheaper than normal roaming rates?

Yes — a roaming bundle for your destination is almost always cheaper than pay-as-you-go roaming rates.

How can I avoid high roaming charges?

Keep data roaming off, use Wi-Fi, and buy a roaming bundle in the My Zong app before you travel.
