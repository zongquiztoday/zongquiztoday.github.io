Zong Quiz Eligibility and Rules

Before you play the Zong daily quiz, it helps to know the rules. Who is allowed to play? How many times can you play each day? What happens if your internet drops in the middle of a round? This guide answers those questions so you can play with confidence and avoid losing a reward over a small mistake.

Who can play?

The quiz is generally open to Zong prepaid users who have the My Zong app installed and are logged in with their Zong number. Some quiz rounds or promotions may be limited to certain users — for example, users in a particular region or on a particular type of account. The app shows whether the quiz is available to you; if it does not appear, it may not be offered on your account at that time.

You need an active Zong SIM and a working internet connection to play. The quiz is played inside the app, so a phone that cannot run the My Zong app cannot play the quiz.

How often can you play?

Most quiz rounds can be played once per day. Once you finish a round, you usually have to wait until the next day for a new set of questions. Some special events allow extra rounds, but the rule for each round is shown on the quiz screen before you start.

Trying to play again by logging out and back in, or by using another phone, does not normally give you a second round — the quiz tracks your number, not just your phone. Play your one round carefully instead of rushing through it.

The main rules to follow

Answer each question within the time limit if the round is timed. Once you select an answer, you usually cannot change it, so read the question fully first. Do not close the app or switch to another app in the middle of a round — the quiz may end, and your progress can be lost. Make sure your internet connection is stable before you start, because a dropped connection can interrupt the round.

Only one account per number is allowed, and the rewards are added to the number you logged in with. Using another person's number or account to play is against the rules and can lead to the reward being cancelled.

Fair play and misuse

Zong's quiz is meant for genuine users. Using automated tools, fake accounts or other tricks to play the quiz is against the rules and can get your account blocked from rewards. The same applies to sharing your login or verification code with others — your verification code is private, and anyone who has it can access your account.

If you see a service that promises to play the quiz for you or guarantee a win in exchange for money or your login details, stay away from it. It is either a scam or a quick way to lose your account.

What if something goes wrong?

If the quiz freezes, your internet drops, or the app closes during a round, wait a few minutes and open the app again. Check the rewards or notifications section to see whether your result was recorded. If the round was lost and you cannot play again that day, you will usually have to wait until the next day — the daily limit is strict.

If a reward you earned is missing, check the app's reward history and your balance first, then call Zong support on 310 with the date, time and score of your round. A screenshot of your result screen helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

Who can play the Zong daily quiz?

Zong prepaid users with the My Zong app installed and logged in. Some rounds may be limited to certain accounts.

How many times can I play the quiz each day?

Usually once per day. The rule for each round is shown on the quiz screen.

What happens if my internet drops during the quiz?

The round may end and your progress can be lost. Play on a stable connection and avoid switching apps mid-round.
