Zong Quiz Not Loading? How to Fix It

You open the My Zong app, tap the quiz — and nothing happens. The quiz screen stays blank, keeps loading, or the app closes. This is a common problem, and in most cases it has a simple fix. This guide walks you through the checks in order, from the quickest to the most thorough.

First: check your internet

The quiz needs an internet connection to load. Check that your phone shows a 4G signal or is connected to Wi-Fi. Try opening a website or another app to see whether the internet is working at all. If your data is finished, the quiz cannot load — check your remaining data with *102# or in the My Zong app.

If you are on mobile data, try switching to Wi-Fi, or move to a place with a stronger signal. A weak signal is one of the most common causes of a quiz that loads halfway and then stops.

Update the app

An old version of the My Zong app may not show the newest quiz at all. Open the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, search for "My Zong", and check whether an update is available. Install it, then close the app fully and open it again. Updates often fix loading problems and add new features, so keeping the app updated is the single most useful habit.

Restart and clear the cache

If the app is updated and the quiz still will not load, close the app completely — not just minimise it — and open it again. If that does not help, restart your phone. A restart clears temporary glitches that can stop an app from loading properly.

On Android, you can also clear the app's cache: go to Settings, then Apps, find My Zong, tap Storage, and tap Clear Cache. Do not tap Clear Data unless you are ready to log in again, because it removes your saved login. On iPhone, the equivalent step is to delete the app and reinstall it from the App Store.

Check whether the quiz is available

Sometimes the quiz itself is the problem, not your phone. The quiz may be paused, the promotion may have ended, or it may not be available on your account at that time. If the rest of the app works fine — your balance and bundles load normally — but the quiz section is missing or blank, the offer may simply not be running.

Wait a day and check again. Quiz promotions change regularly, and a missing quiz today may return tomorrow. You can also ask Zong support on 310 whether the quiz is currently available on your number.

Free up space and check permissions

A phone that is almost full of storage can make apps misbehave. Check your free storage and delete old photos, videos or apps you do not use. Also check that the My Zong app has the permissions it needs — in your phone settings, make sure it is allowed to use mobile data and to send notifications.

If you use a VPN or a data-saving app, try turning it off and loading the quiz again. These tools can interfere with how the app connects to Zong's servers.

When to contact support

If you have tried everything above — strong internet, updated app, restarted phone, cleared cache — and the quiz still will not load after a day or two, call Zong support on 310. Explain what you tried and what happens when you tap the quiz. The agent can check whether the quiz is active on your account and whether there is a known problem with the app.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the Zong quiz not loading?

Usually a weak connection, an outdated app, or the quiz promotion not running. Check your internet and update the app first.

Should I clear the app cache?

On Android, clearing the cache often fixes loading problems. Do not tap Clear Data unless you are ready to log in again.

The quiz is missing from my app. What should I do?

Wait a day — the promotion may have paused — or call 310 to ask whether the quiz is available on your number.
