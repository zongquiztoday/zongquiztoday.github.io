Zong Helpline and Customer Support Numbers

When something goes wrong with your Zong service — a bundle does not activate, a quiz reward is missing, or your balance looks wrong — you need to reach support quickly. This guide lists the official ways to contact Zong and explains how to get your problem solved with the least hassle.

The 310 helpline

The main way to reach Zong support is to call 310 from your Zong number. This connects you to Zong's customer service helpline, where an agent can check your account, your bundles and your balance. The helpline is the right choice for problems that need access to your account, such as a missing bundle, a wrong charge, or a quiz reward that never arrived.

Before you call, have your details ready: your Zong number, the date and time of the problem, and any relevant information such as the name of the bundle or the score of your quiz round. A screenshot of an error message or a result screen is also very helpful. Calling with the facts ready means the agent can help you faster.

Help inside the My Zong app

The My Zong app also has help and support options. Look for a help, support or contact section inside the app, where you may find answers to common questions, a way to report a problem, or a chat option. The app is often the fastest way to solve simple problems yourself: you can check your balance, see active bundles, and view your usage without calling anyone.

If the app itself is the problem — it will not open, will not log in, or shows the wrong information — try updating it, closing it fully and opening it again, or reinstalling it. If that does not help, call 310 and explain what the app is doing.

Visiting a Zong service centre

For problems that cannot be solved by phone, such as SIM replacement, biometric verification, or buying a new SIM, visit a Zong service centre or franchise. Take your original CNIC with you, because most in-person services need identity verification. The staff can also help with packages, new connections and account problems.

Be careful to visit only official Zong service centres or authorised franchises. If someone on the street offers to "fix" your SIM or account for a fee, do not trust them — go to an official centre instead.

What to do about a complaint

If your problem is not solved on the first call, note down the complaint or reference number the agent gives you. Ask when you can expect a reply, and call back with the reference number if nothing happens by then. Keeping a record of dates, times and reference numbers makes a follow-up complaint much easier.

If a charge on your account looks wrong, check the usage and transaction history in the My Zong app first. Sometimes a bundle renewed automatically, or a subscription you forgot about is charging you. The app shows these details, and support can explain any charge you do not recognise.

Staying safe when contacting support

Zong support will never ask you for your password, your verification code, or your bank card details over the phone. Never share those with anyone who calls you claiming to be from Zong. If you receive a suspicious call or message asking for personal details, end the call and report it by calling 310 yourself.

Real support contacts you only through official channels: the 310 helpline, the My Zong app, or an official Zong service centre. Anyone else offering help with your account for money or personal details is almost certainly a scammer.

Frequently asked questions

Call 310 from your Zong number to reach Zong customer support.

Can I get help inside the My Zong app?

Yes — the app has a help or support section, and it also lets you check balance, bundles and usage yourself.

What should I take to a Zong service centre?

Your original CNIC, because most in-person services need identity verification.
