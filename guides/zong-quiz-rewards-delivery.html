How Zong Quiz Rewards Are Delivered

You finished the quiz, you scored well — but where is the reward? Many Zong users ask this question, because rewards do not always appear instantly. This guide explains how quiz rewards are delivered, how long they take, how to check that you received yours, and what to do if it never arrives.

How rewards are delivered

Quiz rewards are added directly to the Zong number you used to log in to the My Zong app. If the reward is free data, it is added to your data balance. If it is free minutes, it is added to your minutes balance. If it is a voucher or discount, it appears in the rewards or offers section of the app. You do not need to claim anything separately — the reward is applied automatically when it is issued.

The reward is always tied to the number you played with. If you have two Zong SIMs, make sure you are logged in with the number you want the reward on, because rewards cannot be moved from one number to another.

How long does delivery take?

Most rewards arrive within a few minutes of finishing the quiz. During busy times — for example, when many people are playing at once — delivery can take a few hours. A delay of a few hours is normal and does not mean the reward is lost.

If more than 24 hours have passed and the reward still has not arrived, it is time to check whether something went wrong. Before that, give the system time: checking every five minutes will not make the reward arrive faster.

How to check your reward

For a data reward, dial *102# to check your remaining data, or look at the balance section of the My Zong app. Compare the amount with what you had before playing. For a minutes reward, check your remaining minutes in the app or by dialling *224# on eligible bundles. For a voucher, look in the rewards or offers section of the app.

Also check the app's notifications. When a reward is added, the app often sends a notification or a message confirming it. If you turned off app notifications, turn them back on so you do not miss these messages.

Why a reward might be delayed or missing

The most common reason is that the score requirement was not met. Each round has a minimum score for the reward, shown on the quiz screen. If you finished below that score, no reward is given — even though you completed the round. Another common reason is a connection problem during the round: if the app lost connection before your result was recorded, the reward may not have been issued.

Rewards also have a validity period. Free data from the quiz often expires within 24 hours, so if you check a day later, the data may have been added and then expired unused. Check promptly after playing, and use free data the same day.

What to do if the reward never arrives

First, confirm that you met the score requirement and that you played in the official My Zong app with the correct number. Check the app's reward history and your balance. Then, if the reward is still missing after 24 hours, call Zong support on 310. Tell them the date and time you played, your score, and the reward that was promised. A screenshot of your quiz result screen makes the complaint much easier to verify.

Keep your expectations fair: quiz rewards are small daily bonuses. If you play every day and collect them promptly, they add up over a month — but no single reward will be large.

Frequently asked questions

How long do Zong quiz rewards take to arrive?

Usually within a few minutes, but sometimes a few hours. Wait 24 hours before reporting a missing reward.

How do I check if I received my quiz reward?

Dial *102# for data, check the My Zong app balance section, or look for a notification in the app.

Why did I not get a reward after finishing the quiz?

Usually the minimum score was not reached, the connection dropped during the round, or the reward expired unused.
