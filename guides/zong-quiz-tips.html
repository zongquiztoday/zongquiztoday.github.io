Zong Quiz Tips: How to Win More Rewards

Winning the Zong daily quiz is not about luck alone. The players who earn rewards most often share a few habits: they prepare a little every day, they read questions carefully, and they manage the timer well. This guide collects the most useful tips to help you score higher and win more rewards.

Build general knowledge every day

Most quiz questions come from general knowledge: Pakistan's geography and history, famous people, sports, science, religion and current affairs. You cannot memorise the daily questions in advance, because they change every day — but you can build the knowledge that answers them.

Spend ten minutes a day reading the news, and keep a small habit of revising school-level general knowledge. Our free practice quiz at the top of the homepage is a good training tool: it uses the same style of multiple-choice questions and tells you the correct answer immediately, so you learn from every mistake.

Read the whole question

The most common reason players lose points is rushing. Quiz questions often include a small word that changes the meaning — "largest", "first", "oldest", "smallest" or a date. Read the full question before looking at the options, and read all four options before choosing.

If two options look similar, look for the detail that separates them. For example, two cities may both be in the same province, but only one is the capital. Slowing down by a few seconds per question is often the difference between a reward and a missed one.

Use elimination

When you are not sure of the answer, eliminate the options that are clearly wrong. In many multiple-choice questions, one or two options are obviously incorrect. Removing them leaves you choosing between two, which doubles your chance of being right.

Also trust your first instinct when you are torn between answers. Players who change their answer at the last second often switch from right to wrong. If you studied a topic and one answer feels familiar, it is usually the right one.

Manage the timer

If the round is timed, do not spend too long on a single hard question. Answer your best guess and move on — the points you gain from the next questions are worth more than the time you would lose. Answer the questions you know quickly, and save your thinking time for the ones you are unsure about.

Play when your connection is strong. A slow connection wastes time loading each question, and a dropped connection can end the round. Wi-Fi or a strong 4G signal is best, and closing other apps before you play helps the quiz run smoothly.

Make it a daily habit

Play every day at the same time, when you are calm and focused — not while walking, driving or doing something else. Check the app's rewards section daily too, so you never miss a free data offer or a special quiz event. Over a month, a steady daily habit earns far more rewards than playing in bursts and forgetting for days.

Finally, keep it fun. The quiz is a short daily game with small rewards. Enjoy the learning, do not stress over one bad round, and come back tomorrow for a fresh set of questions.

Frequently asked questions

How can I score higher in the Zong quiz?

Practise general knowledge daily, read each question fully, eliminate wrong options, and play on a stable connection.

Should I guess if I do not know the answer?

Yes — eliminate the clearly wrong options first, then pick your best guess. A guess can still earn a point.

Does playing every day help?

Yes. Daily play builds knowledge and makes sure you never miss a daily reward.
